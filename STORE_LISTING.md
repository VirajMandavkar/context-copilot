### Detailed Description

**Tired of AI forgetting what you decided 10 prompts ago?**

When working on complex coding projects, content creation, or research across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, long conversations cause models to hallucinate, drift, or forget crucial constraints. Starting a new chat means copying and pasting huge blocks of messy text over and over again.

**Context Copilot fixes this permanently.**

Context Copilot provides a lightweight, distraction-free persistent working memory for your AI conversations. It lives right inside your browser, keeping your critical decisions, constraints, and tasks organized and ready to inject into any prompt with a single click.

---

### Key Features:

⚡ **Instant Highlight & Tagging**
Highlight any snippet in your AI chat and tag it immediately as a **Decision**, **Constraint**, **Task**, or **Note**.

📂 **Non-Intrusive Floating Edge Handle & Sidebar**
Click the sleek edge pill on the right side of your screen or press `Alt + C` to slide open your structured memory. Filter notes by tag, search with keyword queries, and expand/collapse details.

🎯 **1-Click Context Injection**
Starting a new chat or switching models? Click **Inject Context** to compile your organized notes into clean markdown and automatically insert them into the chat's prompt box.

📂 **Custom Groups & Collapsible Accordions (Pro)**
Group your notes by project phase (e.g. "Frontend UI", "API Design", "Deployment") and drag-and-drop cards between groups effortlessly.

🔒 **100% Local & Privacy First**
Your notes and conversations NEVER leave your browser. All data is stored locally in `chrome.storage.local`. Zero tracking, zero telemetry, zero middleman servers.
